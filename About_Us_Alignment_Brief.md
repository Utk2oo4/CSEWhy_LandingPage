## 3. Preparation Shouldn't Be Limited by Geography

### Goal

Remove the large empty space on the right and make the content feel intentionally composed rather than like a long text block.

### Recommended layout

Use a **centered, narrower content container**.

Approximate content width:

`750px–850px`

The entire section should sit centrally within the page, while the text itself can remain left-aligned.

### Structure

```text
                    ABOUT CSEWHY

          Preparation Shouldn't Be
             Limited by Geography

    | UPSC preparation isn't the privilege
    | of a particular city.

    You shouldn't have to leave your home,
    move to Delhi, or spend years navigating
    countless sources just to access quality
    preparation.

    | CSEWhy is built around that belief.

    We bring together PYQs, practice,
    current affairs, courses, study resources,
    and more in one place...
```

### Alignment

- Section container: centered
- Heading: left-aligned
- Body text: left-aligned
- Orange vertical accent lines: retain them
- Do not stretch paragraphs across the full screen
- Avoid excessive right-side whitespace
- Keep comfortable vertical spacing between the two content groups

### Spacing

Use approximately:
- `60–80px` top/bottom section padding
- `24–32px` between heading and first content block
- `28–40px` between the two major content blocks

## 4. More Than Just Resources

### Goal

Make this section feel like a continuation of the story rather than another full-width text document.

Use a **left-aligned, narrower content composition**.

Approximate content width:

`800px–900px`

Do not center every element.

### Structure

```text
More Than Just Resources

| Preparation is not only about collecting material.

It is about knowing what to study, practising consistently,
understanding your mistakes, staying updated, and improving
over time.

That's why CSEWhy is building an ecosystem designed around
the actual preparation journey of an aspirant — from
discovering what to study to practising it, revising it,
and staying connected with what matters.
```

### Alignment

- Section container: aligned with the main page content
- Heading: left-aligned
- Bold statement: left-aligned with orange vertical accent
- Paragraphs: left-aligned
- Keep paragraphs constrained to approximately `800–900px`
- Do not allow text to span the entire viewport
- Avoid a large empty right column

### Spacing

Use approximately:
- `60–70px` top/bottom padding
- `24–28px` below heading
- `20–24px` between paragraphs
- Keep the section compact
